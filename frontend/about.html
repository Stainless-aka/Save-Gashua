WHO WE ARE

About Save Gashua

Community care starts with all of us. Together, we can build a healthier and better-informed community.

OUR STORY

Better health begins with a community that cares.

Save Gashua is a community-led fundraising initiative focused initially on diabetes awareness, screening and support in Gashua, Bade LGA, Yobe State.

We believe that access to health information, early screening and community support can make a meaningful difference in people's lives.

Our initiative aims to bring people together around a shared commitment to improving health awareness and supporting individuals who may need assistance.

Working towards a healthier community.
WHAT WE FOCUS ON

Our areas of focus

Our initial work centres on three connected areas of community health support.

+

Health Awareness

Promoting accessible information about diabetes, prevention and the importance of informed health decisions.

♥

Health Screening

Supporting community screening activities in collaboration with qualified health professionals.

◎

Care Assistance

Working towards practical support for people who may need help accessing diabetes-related care.

OUR APPROACH

Community-led. Professionally guided. Transparently managed.

Save Gashua aims to work with qualified health professionals and trusted community partners to support its activities.

We are committed to describing fundraising activities clearly and communicating how collected funds are used.
