WELCOME TO SAVE GASHUA

A healthier community begins with people who care.

Good health starts with awareness, access to reliable information and the opportunity to receive appropriate care.

Save Gashua is a community-led health initiative bringing people together to support diabetes awareness, early screening and practical assistance for residents who need care.
