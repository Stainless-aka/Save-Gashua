OUR PURPOSE

Our Mission

Making diabetes awareness, screening support and community care more accessible.

WHY WE EXIST

Better awareness. Earlier screening. More accessible support.

Our mission is to make diabetes support more accessible through evidence-based awareness, screening partnerships and transparent, needs-based assistance.

We believe communities should have access to reliable health information and opportunities to understand important health risks.

Through responsible fundraising and collaboration with qualified healthcare professionals, we aim to support practical health initiatives in Gashua, Bade LGA, Yobe State.

Knowledge and access are important parts of community health.
OUR COMMITMENTS

How we intend to make a difference

Our mission is guided by three connected priorities.

01

Evidence-Based Awareness

Promoting reliable information about diabetes, prevention, risk factors and the importance of informed health decisions.

02

Screening Partnerships

Working towards screening opportunities through collaboration with qualified healthcare professionals and trusted partners.

03

Needs-Based Assistance

Supporting individuals based on identified needs while communicating fundraising activities and the use of funds transparently.

OUR PRINCIPLES

Care, collaboration and accountability.

We want our work to be guided by compassion, responsible partnerships and respect for the people we aim to support.

We are committed to clear campaign information and honest communication about fundraising, planned activities and outcomes.
