OUR COMMITMENT

Transparency & Accountability

Trust is built through clear communication, responsible fundraising and honest reporting.

WHY TRANSPARENCY MATTERS

Every contribution deserves accountability.

We intend to publish clear records of contributions received and expenses associated with our programmes.

Supporters should be able to understand how fundraising activities are managed and how funds are intended to support community health initiatives.

We are committed to communicating financial information honestly and distinguishing reported contributions from deposits that have been independently reconciled against bank records.

Responsible reporting builds public trust.
OUR ACCOUNTABILITY PRINCIPLES

How we approach financial reporting

Our reporting approach is built around clarity, verification and privacy.

01

Contribution Records

We intend to maintain clear records of contributions reported by donors and payments received.

02

Verified Deposits

Donor-reported contributions will be distinguished from deposits reconciled against bank records.

03

Programme Expenses

We intend to publish information about programme expenses so supporters can understand how funds are used.

PUBLIC REPORTING

Our financial reports

Financial summaries and programme expense reports will be made available here when reliable records are available.

We will not display estimated totals as confirmed figures or publish financial information that has not been properly reviewed.
